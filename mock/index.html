Jump to a stage
  1. Icons: fuel, drive, garage, fix / wash, gauges, mods, check engine.
  2. A moves the cursor, B selects, C goes back.
  3. Tap the car to see its face and a pop-up wink.
  4. Wait about 15 seconds: it drips oil and the check-engine light blinks.
  5. Garage puts it to sleep. The pop-ups on the shell fold down too.
Sprite sheet

The car up close

Side view on a 64 × 24 pixel grid with separate wheel sprites, so every mod is a layer. The front view is the face: the pop-up headlights are the eyes and the air intake is the grin.

Color on. Limited palette built from NA factory paints.

Color off. 1-bit LCD with dot gaps and a faint segment shadow.

Expressions. Pop-ups up, down, a wink, happy, sleepy, and broken down.
